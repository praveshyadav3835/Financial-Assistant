Privacy Policy — Financial Assistant

Last updated: October 2026

Financial Assistant is a private household finance tool built and used by its two owners (Pravesh Yadav and Divya Misra) to track personal bank transactions, budgets, and investments. It is not offered as a public product or service.

What data this app accesses

With explicit permission, the app's background service connects to specific Gmail inboxes using read-only access, solely to find transactional emails sent by banks and card issuers (such as debit/credit alerts). It does not read, store, or process any other email content, and does not send, delete, or modify any email.

What data is extracted

From matching bank emails, the service extracts only transaction details: amount, date, merchant or payee name, and which account or card was used. The original email content itself is not stored.

Where this data is stored

Extracted transaction data is stored in a private Google Firebase (Firestore) database owned and controlled exclusively by the app's owners. It is not shared with, sold to, or accessible by any third party, advertiser, or external service.

Who can access this data

Only the two individuals who own and use this app. No other person or organization has access to the Gmail connection, the extracted data, or the underlying infrastructure.

Data retention and deletion

Transaction data is retained for as long as the app is in use for personal record-keeping. The Gmail connection can be revoked at any time via Google Account permissions, which immediately stops all further access.
